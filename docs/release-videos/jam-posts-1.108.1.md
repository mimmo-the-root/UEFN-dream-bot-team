## Discord (jam channel)

**Looking for a team for the Epic AI MegaJam? Here it is.** 🤖

Dream Bot Team is a free, open-source kit for UEFN with 22 AI agents that work on your map with you: they plan the game, write and check Verse, test, and polish it.

New in v1.108.1, made for the jam:
- `/concept-card` scores your idea on Epic's 7 judging criteria before you build
- `/devlog` writes your "how AI was used" dev log from your real session logs
- `/jam-check` checks the rules before you submit
- Start a new map and the kit installs itself with one yes

Jam: Nov 12-16. Grab the kit, build something fun, and tell us what you make.
