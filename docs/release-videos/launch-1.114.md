## Discord announcement

**Dream Bot Team 1.114: the console grew up.**

Free, open-source kit for UEFN with 22 AI agents. What is new:
- One design system for all eight console pages: colour means status, light animations, aligned numbers, readable text. `ui_lint` checks it.
- The Docs page shows your turn as a first-class step, with exact instructions and a box only you can tick.
- A shorter, honest README: what is proven on a real editor, what is not, and when you do not need the kit.
- `/spec`, `/game-plan`, `/task-list`, `/consistency`, `/environment`, `/audit`, `/delivery`: from an idea to a delivery gate.

Try the pilot demo in a fresh empty project (`/demo` or `/demo --auto`). Found a problem? Open an issue with what you expected and what happened.
https://github.com/mimmo-the-root/UEFN-dream-bot-team
