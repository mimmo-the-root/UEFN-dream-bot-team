# Background music for the console

Put audio files here and switch **♪ Music** on in the header of any console page (next to the Aura colours):
they play one after the other, in a loop, while you work.

- Formats: `.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.mp4`, `.ogg`, `.wav` (only the sound of an `.mp4` is played).
- Order: alphabetical by file name (start a name with `01 `, `02 `... to choose the order).
- The switch, the volume slider, the track and the position are remembered by your browser, so moving to another
  console page continues the music. A browser may ask for one click after a page change: the button then says **Resume**.
- Files you add stay yours: a kit update never removes or replaces them.
- `Nocturnal Focus.mp3` ships with the kit (made by the kit's owner with an AI music tool). Delete it if you do not want it.
