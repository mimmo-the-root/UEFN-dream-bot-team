---
description: Score a game idea on Epic's seven jam criteria plus feasibility and scope (out of 100) and write the concept card
---

Ask the owner for the one-sentence pitch if it was not given. Score nine criteria from 1 to 5 yourself and say in one short line why for each. Seven are Epic's judging criteria: `ambition` (Ambition and Innovation), `theme` (Use of Contest Theme; leave it out until the theme is announced; 1 means it ignores the theme, which the rules do not allow), `fun` (Fun Factor), `gameplay`, `visuals`, `level` (Level Design), `audio`. Score what the idea can realistically reach, not a dream. Two are ours: `feasibility` (what UEFN and Verse can really do: check the matching skills and the second brain first) and `scope` (the days left; 5 means small and easy to finish). Then run `python Claude/hooks/concept_card.py --title "..." --pitch "..." --scores ambition=N,theme=N,fun=N,gameplay=N,visuals=N,level=N,audio=N,feasibility=N,scope=N --days-left N` (on Windows use `py -3` if `python` is not found). Show the result. Write it with `--write` only after the owner says yes.
