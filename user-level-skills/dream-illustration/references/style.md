# Style of the Dream Bot Team pictures

- Paper: cream (#f7f0df) with a light grain. Ink strokes (#1d1b3a), 4 px, round caps, a slight wobble filter so lines look hand-drawn.
- Colours: violet #7c4dff, pink #ff6fb1, mint #35d6a0, sun #ffc933, sky #7cc8ff, red #ff5a5f. Colour has meaning like in the console: mint done, sun needs you, pink danger or stop, sky info.
- Fonts: Caveat Brush for titles, Patrick Hand for small words.
- Characters: small round-faced robots (one per agent, each with a prop: pencil, clipboard), a curious kid for the owner, chickens and a farm for the pilot map.
- Voice: simple words a curious child understands, aimed at professionals. Title in capitals, one closing sentence ("The bigger the consequence, the harder the gate.").
- Show the magic, not graphs: a thing that moves, glows or changes, never a chart.
- The picture must have no text overlapping a shape. Check by looking.
