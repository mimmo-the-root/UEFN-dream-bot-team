# Levels and cameras

Starting levels (adjust by ear): ambience lowest, music a step above, effects clear above music, voice or announcements on top.

When to use a special camera
- Intro or outro of a round: short, for everyone, then released.
- Spectating after elimination: personal to the eliminated player.
- Fixed view maps (puzzle, platform from the side): set at the start and keep for the whole match.

Checks before publishing
- Join in the middle of a round: is the camera right?
- Eliminate and respawn: is the camera back to normal?
- Mute everything but effects: are the key events still understandable?
- Walk away from every loud source: does it fade?
