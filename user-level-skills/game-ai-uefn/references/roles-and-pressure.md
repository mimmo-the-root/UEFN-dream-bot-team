# Roles and pressure

| Role | Behavior wanted | Design check |
|---|---|---|
| Pressure | comes straight at the player | few at a time, readable approach path |
| Area | stays near a place | the place is worth defending, the exit is clear |
| Target | the player hunts it | it moves in a pattern, reward on elimination |

Pressure numbers
- Time for one player to defeat one enemy (seconds).
- Enemies active together near one player (keep small).
- Damage the player takes per second from all of them together.
Make the first two grow slowly and keep the third inside what the player can survive and heal.

Before publishing: play with the full player count, with a weak player and with a strong one, and note when the pressure feels flat or too much.
