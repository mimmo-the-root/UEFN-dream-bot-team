## It starts with a feeling

You are fifteen. It is late, the house is quiet, and there is a map in your head.

A farm at sunset. Chickens running wild between the fences. A countdown. Your friends screaming "one more round!" The whole thing is so clear you can almost hear it.

And between that feeling and a game you can actually play, there is a wall: code, menus, hundreds of settings, and the fear that one wrong click breaks everything.

Most dreams stop at that wall.

## Someone stood in front of it, alone

Fifty islands to look after. Not enough hours. Not enough hands.

He could have given up on the dream. He built a team instead.

## Meet the team

**Dream Bot Team** is twenty-two AI specialists, and every one of them has exactly one job.

One writes the code. Two others try to break it before you ever see it. One keeps the plan honest. One guards the rules. One knows every tool of the editor by heart, and has proven each trick on a live editor, not on paper.

They do not guess. They ask the editor, they read the answer back, they show you the proof. When they are not sure, they stop and ask *you*. Nothing is ever deleted behind your back. Nothing goes out into the world until your own hand says yes.

You stay the one with the dream. They do the heavy lifting.

## And it is alive

Open the console and look.

Every agent lights up while it works. Steps turn green one by one. The whole thing breathes, because a system you can see living is a system you can trust.

## It grows up with you

Every map teaches the team something. A mistake made once is never made twice. A trick that worked becomes a recipe. The next map starts smarter than the last one ended.

Day after day you watch it get better, because you are the one raising it. It is not a tool you rent. It is a team that becomes yours.

## Do not believe us. Watch.

```text
/demo --auto
```

Lean back. In a few minutes a game appears: a farm, chickens, a pen, a score, a countdown, a winner.

You did not write a line of code.

You watched it happen, like a curious kid who just found out what is possible.

That is the whole promise. Everything else is detail.

## Take it

Free. Open source. MIT license. Yours to use for one island or fifty.

**Dream. Describe. Watch it come alive.**
