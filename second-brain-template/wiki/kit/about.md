# About

## Who made this

Dream Bot Team for UEFN is built by **Mimmo_the_root**, to solve one problem: working with AI tools across about fifty islands without losing track of any of them. Everything here was designed through that lens.

A few setup steps are manual on purpose. Not because they could not be scripted, but because you should see what is being installed on your computer instead of having it hidden behind a one-click installer.
